Welcome to the infopage of the **Freshwater Systems Science Workshop**.

We are an informal and open community of interdisciplinary scientists broadly working on research topics relating to the interconnections between humans, ecosystems, Earth systems, and freshwater systems. Our work spans all components of the hydrological cycle, including surface water, soil moisture, groundwater, atmospheric water, and frozen water, their interconnections, and their complex and multi-scale connections to people and ecosystems. Our purpose emerges from a collective realisation that the best freshwater resilience work is possible when working within a diverse, engaged, and supportive community of thematically-aligned collaborators. 

**Group perspective paper:** [Orientations to navigate the complexity of freshwater resilience in social-ecological systems](https://doi.org/10.31223/X5280S). Preprint on *EarthArXiv*.
